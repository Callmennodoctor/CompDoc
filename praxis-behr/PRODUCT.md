## Platform

web

## Stack
Webflow (existing site "Praxis Antonia Behr", ID 6a2135bd7da18f68d3f5142d, pages: Home, Über mich, Leistungen, Anmeldung & Kontakt, Aktuelles). Final build happens in Webflow; design is created first in Claude Design.

## Users
Primary: parents in Berlin, usually evening, on a phone, worried and tired, often after a hint from school or pediatrician and after several therapist rejections. Job: find out quickly whether this practice fits, whether a place is free, what it costs, how to get in touch.
Secondary: adolescents and young adults 14 to 21 checking privately whether therapy and this therapist feel safe for them. Tertiary: referrers (pediatricians, schools, youth services).

## Product Purpose
Website of a private psychotherapy practice (Kinder- und Jugendlichenpsychotherapie) of Antonia Behr in Berlin. Success: a qualified first phone contact from families who fit, fewer unfit requests, transparent capacity.

## Positioning
Single therapist private practice for children, adolescents and young adults up to 21 and their families. Open: Bezirk, therapy method (VT/TP/AP/systemic), exact legal job title, GKV reimbursement route (§ 13 Abs. 3 SGB V) yes/no. [INFERRED from existing Webflow SEO texts: Privatpraxis, approbierte KJP.]

## Operating Context
Contact via phone hours and a minimal callback form. Capacity changes often (waitlist open/closed). Legal frame: Berufsordnung Psychotherapeutenkammer Berlin, Heilmittelwerbegesetz, DSGVO Art. 9.

## Capabilities and Constraints
No emergency service; crisis hints required on every page. No symptom free text in forms. Capacity status must be CMS editable.

## Brand Commitments
Name "Praxis Antonia Behr" (spelling to be confirmed, the user also wrote "Bär"). German, Sie for parents, Du for adolescents.

## Evidence on Hand
No photos, no logo, no bio yet. Must not fabricate testimonials, success rates, credentials, addresses or waiting times.

## Product Principles
1. Clarity for an exhausted parent beats expression.
2. Adolescents must feel addressed as near adults, not as children.
3. Honesty about capacity and cost is the trust signal.
4. Sober, professional, never selling.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA, plain language, parents with limited German, reduced motion respected.
